Bug fixes were needed for route optimization failures, edit/delete buttons, and alias display.
For Agenda, the core requirement is Google Calendar API integration, allowing display of saved routes, manual reordering, individual time/duration editing, a full calendar visualization with commercial hours, Google-like color themes, and proportional time adjustment. A Criar Evento button for manual event creation is also explicitly requested.
