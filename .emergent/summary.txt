<product_requirements>
The SuperDry laundry business requires a comprehensive management system with 15 modules, including Dashboard, Clients, Budgets, Catalog, Budget Config, System Config, and a crucial Rotas (Routes) module. Key features include unique ID generation, diverse measurement types, complex status flows, Google Maps/Calendar API integration, PDF generation, WhatsApp communication, audit system, and permissions, all responsive for PC and mobile.

Phase 1 implemented Dashboard, full CRUD for Clients (with Google Places/Geocoding, mini-maps), Catalog, and Budgets (with editing, quick client add, WhatsApp/PDF generation). New modules Budget Config (editable risk texts, general conditions) and System Config (company details, LGPD, hybrid address search, mini-map) were also built.

The current focus is Phase 2: **Rotas (Routes) module**. This module needs four sections: Serviços disponíveis para rota, Rota Carro, Rota Moto, and Otimização de Rota. It should display services from Orçamentos with statuses Retirar, Executar No Local, Entregar, allow selection of vehicle type (car/moto), and move items to respective route sections. The Otimização de Rota section (later renamed Priorização de Rota) should receive items, allow setting priorities (PRIMEIRA, AUTOMÁTICA, ÚLTIMA), and display a map. The map must be centered on Cuiabá, start and end at the company address, show numbered markers for stops, and display the optimized route visually. It must also include two buttons, ROTA CURTA and ROTA EFICIENTE, to calculate routes based on shortest distance or efficiency (considering traffic/side of road). Route stops have standard durations (30 min for Retirar/Entregar, 120 min for Executar No Local), with an additional 60 min for merged stops at the same address. Routes need unique IDs (RTE-000000) and stops (STP-000000), and a Visualizar e Salvar section that is *always visible* showing the optimized route, map, and summary, with options to save.
